## ✨ Reflection 
---
> The two industry talks from Clarity Techworks and AirAsia have given me a more clear and more practical idea of how ICT has something important to offer in the modern industries. Ranging from fintech to travel to digital technologies, it is not merely the world of programming as I’ve earlier perceived it.

>At Clarity Techworks, for instance, I learned about the role ICT professionals play in assisting with the administration of real financial systems with the help of the power of the Internet through the use of the mobile application,blockchain, and web application interfaces. Clearly, it’s about precision, security, and conscientiousness in dealing with hard money situations or building trust with the users.At Air Asia, we learned about the role ICT professionals play in empowering a large organization to transform itself to a digital platform by connecting the interfaces of the services we provide like travel, deliveries, and fintech under one system.

>The two presentations also inspired me to be a proactive and independent learner since technology changes so fast, especially with regards to Web 3 innovations, mobile software development, cloud computing, data processing, etc. I realized that ICT professionals must constantly upgrade their skills, adapt to new tools, and stay relevant in a competitive industry. Most importantly, I learned that ICT jobs are meaningful because they solve real-world problems, improve efficiency, and create better experiences for users and businesses globally.

## 🌟 Suggestion for Improvement 
---

>• Providing additional real life project cases or failure stories might increase students’ comprehension of real life problems.

## ✅ Conclusion
---
> In conclusion , both the career talks from Clarity Techworks and AirAsia demonstrate that ICT is vital in driving innovation, efficiency, and digital transformation across industries. Whether operating under Fintech, Blockchain, or Travel & Digital Services, ICT professionals construct systems that not only connect people but also Businesses with Technology.

> The talks really reminded me that success in ICT requires technical skills, adaptability, communication, discipline, and continuous learning. Overall, these sessions inspired me to take my studies more seriously, build practical skills, and prepare myself for a responsible and impactful future in the ICT field.
