## ✅ Conclusion
---
> This design thinking project shows how essential of understanding real user needs before developing solutions . By applying the phase of empathising, defining, ideating, prototyping, and testing, we were able to identify key problems faced by many students. Overall, the project showed that design thinking is an effective approach for solving real-life problems in a creative , efficient and structured way . It encourages our teamwork skils , communication skils , critical thinking and continuous improvement based on user feedback . This experience can be the key for our future reference to become a good employer that effectively contribute to the team , company and industries .
